Game of Thrones - Season 3 Summary

Overview
Season 3 covers the shifting tides of the War of the Five Kings. With Stannis Baratheon defeated at the Blackwater, the Lannisters hold a firm grip on King's Landing through their new alliance with House Tyrell. Meanwhile, Robb Stark's Northern campaign begins to unravel, and Daenerys Targaryen seeks a powerful army in the East.

King's Landing
Tywin Lannister is now officially the Hand of the King and exerts total control over the capital and his family. To secure the Lannister legacy and alliances, Tywin orchestrates political marriages: he arranges for King Joffrey to marry Margaery Tyrell, forces his son Tyrion to marry Sansa Stark (preventing the Tyrells or Northerners from claiming Winterfell), and plots to marry Cersei off to Loras Tyrell. Tyrion and Sansa's marriage is miserable, though Tyrion treats her with kindness and refuses to consummate the union against her will.

The Riverlands and The Red Wedding
Robb Stark, the King in the North, is losing momentum. After his mother, Catelyn, secretly releases the valuable hostage Jaime Lannister (tasking Brienne of Tarth with escorting him to King's Landing in exchange for Sansa and Arya), Robb's men grow rebellious. Robb executes Lord Karstark for treason, causing the Karstark forces to abandon him.

Desperate for troops, Robb attempts to rebuild his broken alliance with the prickly Lord Walder Frey. Since Robb broke his vow to marry a Frey girl, he offers his uncle, Edmure Tully, to marry Roslin Frey instead. The wedding takes place at the Twins, but it is a trap secretly orchestrated by Tywin Lannister, Walder Frey, and Robb's own bannerman, Roose Bolton. In a massacre known as the "Red Wedding," Robb, his pregnant wife Talisa, Catelyn Stark, and most of the Northern army are brutally murdered. Roose Bolton personally stabs Robb and is subsequently named the new Warden of the North by the Lannisters.

Dragonstone
Following his crushing defeat at the Blackwater, a depressed Stannis Baratheon secludes himself. The red priestess Melisandre acquires Gendry, King Robert's bastard son, and uses leeches filled with his "king's blood" in a ritual to curse Stannis's three rival kings: Robb Stark, Balon Greyjoy, and Joffrey Baratheon. Horrified by Melisandre's plan to sacrifice Gendry in the fires, Davos Seaworth smuggles the boy to safety. Stannis sentences Davos to death for this treason, but Davos presents a desperate letter from the Night's Watch begging for help against the impending threat of the White Walkers. Stannis realizes his true duty lies in the North.

The North and Beyond the Wall
Jon Snow successfully infiltrates the wildling army led by Mance Rayder, the "King-Beyond-the-Wall." Jon falls in love with the wildling Ygritte, breaking his Night's Watch vows. He climbs the massive ice Wall with the wildling raiding party, but eventually betrays them to warn the Night's Watch of the impending attack, leaving a heartbroken Ygritte behind.

Meanwhile, Bran Stark discovers he is a "Warg" (able to enter the minds of animals) and has a crucial destiny. He travels north beyond the Wall with Jojen and Meera Reed to find the mysterious Three-Eyed Raven.

Elsewhere in the North, Theon Greyjoy is held captive and subjected to unimaginable physical and psychological torture by the sadistic Ramsay Snow, the bastard son of Roose Bolton. Ramsay systematically strips away Theon's identity, renaming him "Reek."

Across the Narrow Sea (Essos)
Daenerys Targaryen arrives in Slaver's Bay seeking an army. In the city of Astapor, she agrees to trade one of her growing dragons for an army of 8,000 elite, eunuch warriors known as the Unsullied. However, during the transaction, she commands the dragon to burn the cruel slave masters and orders the Unsullied to sack the city and free all the slaves. She asks the Unsullied to fight for her as free men, and they willingly agree. Daenerys then marches on the city of Yunkai, defeating its mercenary forces and freeing its slaves as well, earning the title "Mhysa" (Mother) from the liberated people.
