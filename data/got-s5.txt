Game of Thrones - Season 5 Summary

Overview
Season 5 sees power vacuums being filled across Westeros and Essos. With Tywin Lannister dead, King's Landing is vulnerable to religious extremism. In the North, the Boltons consolidate their ruthless power, while the impending threat of the White Walkers becomes undeniable for those at the Wall. Meanwhile, Daenerys Targaryen faces fierce rebellion in Meereen.

King's Landing and Dorne
Following Tywin's death, Cersei Lannister acts as Queen Regent for her youngest son, the new King Tommen. Feeling threatened by Tommen's new wife, Margaery Tyrell, Cersei seeks to undermine the Tyrells by empowering a zealot religious group known as the Faith Militant, led by the charismatic High Sparrow. The Faith Militant arrests Margaery's brother, Loras, for his homosexuality, and then arrests Margaery for perjuring herself to protect him. However, Cersei's plan backfires when the High Sparrow also arrests her for her past sins (including her incestuous affair with her cousin Lancel). To secure her release and await trial, Cersei is forced to endure a humiliating, naked "Walk of Atonement" through the streets of King's Landing, returning to the Red Keep broken but vengeful.

In the southern region of Dorne, Ellaria Sand (the paramour of the late Oberyn Martell) seeks revenge against the Lannisters. Jaime Lannister and Bronn travel secretly to Dorne to rescue Jaime's daughter, Princess Myrcella. Though they seemingly broker peace with Doran Martell and secure Myrcella's return, Ellaria secretly poisons the young princess with a fatal kiss on the docks. Myrcella dies in Jaime's arms on a ship bound for King's Landing.

The North
Petyr "Littlefinger" Baelish brokers a marriage between Sansa Stark and the sadistic Ramsay Bolton to solidify Bolton control over the North, while secretly hoping to position Sansa to eventually retake her home. Sansa returns to a Winterfell occupied by her family's betrayers and suffers horrific physical and psychological abuse at Ramsay's hands. She discovers Theon Greyjoy (still acting as the broken "Reek") is there. Eventually, Theon's fractured identity heals just enough to help Sansa; together, they escape Winterfell by leaping from the high castle walls into the deep snow.

Meanwhile, Stannis Baratheon marches his army south from the Wall toward Winterfell. However, his forces are bogged down by a severe blizzard. In a desperate and horrifying move to secure the Lord of Light's favor and melt the snows, Stannis allows Melisandre to burn his young daughter, Shireen, alive at the stake. This atrocity causes his wife to hang herself and half his army to desert. The remains of his forces are easily slaughtered by the Bolton cavalry. A wounded Stannis is found on the battlefield and executed by Brienne of Tarth, who finally avenges Renly Baratheon.

The Wall
Jon Snow is elected Lord Commander of the Night's Watch, much to the dismay of the older, conservative members. Recognizing that the wildlings will just become soldiers for the Night King's undead army if left beyond the Wall, Jon makes the highly controversial decision to ally with them. He travels to the wildling encampment at Hardhome to evacuate them. During the evacuation, the White Walkers and their massive army of wights launch a devastating surprise attack. Jon barely escapes on a boat, watching in horror as the Night King resurrects the thousands of dead wildlings.

Returning to Castle Black with the surviving wildlings, Jon faces deep resentment from his own men. A faction of mutineers, led by Alliser Thorne and young Olly, lure Jon into a trap and take turns stabbing him to death, muttering "For the Watch." Jon is left bleeding out in the snow.

Across the Narrow Sea (Essos)
In Braavos, Arya Stark arrives at the House of Black and White to join the Faceless Men, a guild of mystical assassins. She struggles to abandon her identity and become "no one." When she spots Meryn Trant (a man on her kill list) in Braavos, she steals a face from the guild's vault to brutally assassinate him. For taking a life not owed to the Many-Faced God, she is punished by being struck blind.

Tyrion Lannister, fleeing Westeros, travels with Varys toward Meereen. He is kidnapped by a disgraced Jorah Mormont, who hopes to win back Daenerys's favor by presenting Tyrion to her. During their journey, they are attacked by "Stone Men," and Jorah secretly contracts the fatal disease greyscale. They eventually reach Meereen and are bought as slaves to fight in the fighting pits, where they reunite with Daenerys. She accepts Tyrion as an advisor but banishes Jorah again.

Daenerys's rule in Meereen is plagued by a violent insurgency group known as the Sons of the Harpy. During a grand gladiatorial event at Daznak's Pit, the Sons of the Harpy launch a massive, coordinated assassination attempt on Daenerys and her supporters. Just as they are cornered, her largest dragon, Drogon, arrives and incinerates the attackers. Daenerys climbs onto Drogon's back and flies away. She is dropped in the middle of the Dothraki Sea, where she is quickly surrounded by a massive, hostile Dothraki khalasar. Back in Meereen, Tyrion, Varys, Missandei, and Grey Worm are left to govern the volatile city in her absence.
