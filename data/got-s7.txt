Game of Thrones - Season 7 Summary

Overview
Season 7 accelerates the pace as the major players finally converge. Daenerys Targaryen arrives in Westeros to claim the Iron Throne, but the true war looms in the North. Jon Snow desperately seeks allies to fight the Army of the Dead, forcing bitter enemies to come face-to-face and make impossible choices.

Dragonstone and the War for Westeros
Daenerys Targaryen lands at Dragonstone, her ancestral home, along with her dragons, her massive army, and her Hand, Tyrion Lannister. However, her conquest hits early roadblocks. Cersei Lannister, now Queen on the Iron Throne, allies with the ruthless Euron Greyjoy. Euron destroys Daenerys's naval fleet, capturing Yara Greyjoy, Ellaria Sand, and her daughter Tyene (whom Cersei later poisons in a dungeon to avenge Myrcella).

Meanwhile, Jaime Lannister leads the Lannister army to sack Highgarden, eliminating Daenerys's Tyrell allies. Before dying by poison, Olenna Tyrell confesses to Jaime that she was the one who murdered King Joffrey. In retaliation for these losses, Daenerys ambushes the Lannister army on their way back to King's Landing. Riding Drogon and backed by her Dothraki horde, she decimates the Lannister forces and destroys their supply train, narrowly avoiding being killed by a giant scorpion ballista operated by Bronn.

The North and Winterfell
Jon Snow travels to Dragonstone at the invitation of Daenerys, who demands he bend the knee. Jon refuses, focused entirely on the threat of the White Walkers, and instead asks to mine the dragonglass beneath Dragonstone to make weapons. Though initially distrustful, a mutual respect and eventual romance begin to bloom between Jon and Daenerys.

Back at Winterfell, Sansa Stark rules the North in Jon's absence. She is soon reunited with her surviving siblings, Bran and Arya. Bran is now entirely consumed by his role as the detached, mystical Three-Eyed Raven, while Arya has become a lethal Faceless assassin. Petyr "Littlefinger" Baelish attempts to drive a wedge between Sansa and Arya to increase his own influence. However, the Stark children see through his manipulations. In a satisfying trial, Sansa lists Littlefinger's many crimes and betrayals (including his betrayal of Ned Stark), and Arya executes him by cutting his throat.

Beyond the Wall
To convince Cersei Lannister to call a truce and join the fight against the dead, Jon Snow leads a dangerous expedition beyond the Wall to capture a live wight. The group, including The Hound, Jorah Mormont, and Gendry, succeeds in capturing a wight but becomes trapped on a frozen lake surrounded by the Night King's massive army.

Daenerys flies her three dragons north to rescue them. While she manages to save most of the group, the Night King hurls an ice spear and kills one of her dragons, Viserion. A heartbroken Daenerys escapes with the survivors, while Jon is left behind and saved at the last minute by his undead uncle, Benjen Stark. Aboard a ship heading south, a recovering Jon agrees to bend the knee and recognizes Daenerys as his Queen.

The Dragonpit Summit
The major factions meet at the ruins of the Dragonpit in King's Landing. Jon and The Hound present the captured wight, horrifying Cersei. She agrees to a truce and pledges the Lannister army to the Great War in the North. However, she later reveals to Jaime that she is lying; she plans to let the monsters and the Northerners destroy each other while she uses the Golden Company mercenary group to secure her grip on Westeros. Disgusted by her betrayal and broken promises, Jaime finally abandons Cersei and rides North alone.

The Fall of the Wall
As Jon and Daenerys travel North together by ship, they consummate their relationship. Intercut with this, Bran Stark and Samwell Tarly piece together the truth of Jon's parentage. They realize that Rhaegar Targaryen and Lyanna Stark were legally married, meaning Jon Snow is not a bastard at all. His real name is Aegon Targaryen, making him the true heir to the Iron Throne—and Daenerys's nephew.

The season concludes with a terrifying climax at the Wall. The Night King, riding the resurrected, undead dragon Viserion, attacks the fortress of Eastwatch-by-the-Sea. Viserion breathes blue fire, shattering the ancient, magical ice Wall and allowing the Army of the Dead to march into Westeros.
