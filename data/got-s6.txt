Game of Thrones - Season 6 Summary

Overview
Season 6 resolves many of the massive cliffhangers from the previous season, focusing on the resurgence of House Stark in the North and Cersei Lannister's explosive power grab in King's Landing. Meanwhile, Daenerys Targaryen finally completes her preparations in Essos and begins her long-awaited journey to Westeros.

The North and The Wall
Following Jon Snow's assassination, his loyalists (including Davos Seaworth) barricade themselves with his body, surrounded by mutineers. A desperate Davos convinces the red priestess Melisandre to attempt a resurrection ritual. Miraculously, Jon gasps back to life. He executes the mutineers (including Alliser Thorne and Olly) by hanging, and having technically fulfilled his vow to serve the Night's Watch until his death, Jon declares his watch ended and leaves the order.

Shortly after, Sansa Stark arrives at Castle Black with Brienne of Tarth, reuniting with Jon. She convinces him to help her retake Winterfell from the sadistic Ramsay Bolton. They travel the North gathering a small, ragtag army of wildlings and smaller Northern houses. The armies clash in the brutal "Battle of the Bastards." Ramsay uses Jon's youngest brother, Rickon (whom Ramsay captured), as bait, killing the boy just before Jon can reach him. Jon's forces are eventually surrounded and nearly crushed, but Petyr "Littlefinger" Baelish arrives at the last minute with the Knights of the Vale (arranged secretly by Sansa), decimating the Bolton army. Jon beats Ramsay nearly to death, and Sansa later feeds Ramsay to his own starving hounds. The Northern lords gather at Winterfell and declare Jon Snow the new "King in the North."

Beyond the Wall
Bran Stark trains with the Three-Eyed Raven, exploring visions of the past. He accidentally allows the Night King to mark him in a vision, breaking the magical protections of their cave. The White Walkers and an army of wights attack. Bran's direwolf Summer, the Three-Eyed Raven, and the Children of the Forest are killed. In a tragic and mind-bending sequence, Bran's gentle giant companion, Hodor, sacrifices himself to hold a door against the wights, revealing that a time-loop vision caused Hodor's mental disability when he was a boy (compressing "Hold the door" into "Hodor").

Bran escapes with Meera Reed and is rescued by his undead uncle, Benjen Stark. Through another vision, Bran witnesses the events at the Tower of Joy during Robert's Rebellion, discovering Jon Snow's true parentage: Jon is not Ned Stark's bastard, but the son of Ned's sister, Lyanna Stark, and Rhaegar Targaryen.

King's Landing
The High Sparrow continues to manipulate King Tommen, effectively merging the Crown and the Faith. Cersei Lannister is set to face trial for her crimes, but she knows she will likely lose. On the day of her trial, Cersei orchestrates a catastrophic plot: using caches of highly volatile "wildfire" hidden beneath the city centuries ago by the Mad King, she blows up the Great Sept of Baelor. This explosive trap instantly kills the High Sparrow, Margaery Tyrell, Loras Tyrell, Mace Tyrell, and Kevan Lannister, wiping out all her immediate rivals.

Watching from the Red Keep, King Tommen is so horrified by the destruction and the death of his wife that he steps out of his window, falling to his death. With all of her children now dead (fulfilling an old prophecy), Cersei Lannister crowns herself Queen of the Seven Kingdoms and sits on the Iron Throne.

Braavos and The Riverlands
In Braavos, Arya Stark trains as a blind beggar until her sight is restored. However, she ultimately refuses to assassinate a sympathetic actress, rejecting the Faceless Men's ideology. The waif is sent to kill her, but Arya lures her into the dark, kills her, and adds her face to the Hall of Faces. She boldly tells Jaqen H'ghar, "I am Arya Stark of Winterfell, and I'm going home." She travels to the Riverlands, disguises herself as a servant, and assassinates Lord Walder Frey (after feeding him his own sons in a pie) to avenge the Red Wedding.

Elsewhere, it is revealed that Sandor "The Hound" Clegane survived his wounds from Season 4. After a rogue sect of the Brotherhood Without Banners slaughters a peaceful community he joined, The Hound seeks revenge and ends up joining the main Brotherhood, led by Beric Dondarrion and Thoros of Myr, heading North to fight the dead.

Essos (Meereen and the Dothraki Sea)
Daenerys is taken as a prisoner to Vaes Dothrak, the sacred city of the Dothraki, where all khals gather. When the khals threaten to rape her or force her to live out her days in a temple of widows, she tips over burning braziers, trapping them inside their temple and burning them all alive. Once again emerging unburnt from the flames, she forces the entire Dothraki horde to bow to her.

She returns to Meereen on the back of Drogon just as the slave masters from other cities are laying siege to it. Unleashing all three of her dragons simultaneously for the first time, she decimates the masters' fleet. Theon and Yara Greyjoy arrive from the Iron Islands (fleeing their murderous uncle Euron) and pledge their remaining ships to Daenerys. With a massive armada, a Dothraki horde, an army of Unsullied, three full-grown dragons, and Tyrion Lannister as her named Hand of the Queen, Daenerys Targaryen finally sets sail across the Narrow Sea to conquer Westeros.
