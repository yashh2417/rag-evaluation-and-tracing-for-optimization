House of the Dragon - Season 3 Summary

Overview
Season 3 escalates the Dance of the Dragons from political maneuvering and skirmishes to a catastrophic, full-scale continental war. The season focuses on the devastating casualties of the conflict and the realization that power is much easier to acquire than to control. The dragons themselves increasingly stop acting as obedient military assets and become unpredictable, ancient forces of devastation with their own agency.

The Battle of the Gullet and Dragon Moves
The season opens with Rhaenyra planning to attack King's Landing, trusting in Alicent Hightower's secret peace offer. However, a deeply suspicious Prince Jacaerys believes it to be a trap. Taking matters into his own hands, Jace locks his mother in her room and flies with Baela to support the Velaryon fleet. This ignites the Battle of the Gullet, a massive and bloody naval clash that forever alters the war at sea. Meanwhile, in the Vale, a weak and desperate Rhaena Targaryen finally manages to bond with the wild, unpredictable dragon Sheepstealer.

King's Landing and the Black Queen
In the capital, chaos reigns when the Green Council discovers that King Aegon and Larys Strong have secretly fled. Aemond Targaryen promptly declares himself King. Alicent, honoring her secret pact with Rhaenyra, manipulates the board: she forges letters to stall the advancing Hightower army and convinces Aemond to take Vhagar to Harrenhal to hunt down Daemon. With the city's heaviest defenses gone, Rhaenyra swoops in and successfully takes King's Landing. However, her triumph as she sits on the Iron Throne is incredibly short-lived. "Queen's Landing" is immediately plagued by unrest, a lack of funds, and the outright refusal of the Faith to formally crown her.

The Riverlands and The Butcher's Ball
In the Riverlands, Daemon Targaryen's forces—now heavily bolstered by the arrival of the fierce Northern "Winter Wolves" led by Roderick Dustin—crush the Westerlands army, with Dustin personally beheading Jason Lannister. While Aemond is distracted at Harrenhal, Ser Criston Cole is left leading a vulnerable Green army. In a brutal, muddy ambush known as the Butcher's Ball, Cole's host is decimated, and the Lord Commander meets a grim end, dying without the glorious, heroic song he always envisioned for himself.

Aegon's Fate and the Dragonseeds' Betrayal
While fleeing in a covered wagon, Aegon and Larys Strong are intercepted by soldiers of House Staunton, who are loyal to Rhaenyra. To avoid being executed on the spot, Larys reveals the King's true identity, ensuring they are kept alive as valuable hostages to be shipped to Dragonstone. Later, a battered Aegon eventually manages to reunite with his equally maimed dragon, Sunfyre.

Meanwhile, Rhaenyra's greatest advantage—her new, lowborn dragonriders—begins to fracture. The dragonseeds, particularly Ulf the White, prove incredibly difficult to control, openly destabilizing Rhaenyra's war effort and treating their immense power with dangerous arrogance.

The Treasons at Tumbleton
The war expands into a complete national disaster as Alicent's youngest son, Daeron Targaryen, officially enters the fray on his dragon, Tessarion, marching with the Hightower army from the Reach. The climax of the season occurs during the First Battle of Tumbleton. In the season finale, "The Treasons at Tumbleton," devastating betrayals from the dragonseeds fundamentally shatter the balance of power. The ensuing carnage proves that the war has grown beyond anyone's control, with dragons inflicting unimaginable destruction on all involved.
